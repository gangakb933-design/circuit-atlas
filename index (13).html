Ohm's Law

Enter any two values and the third is worked out. Leave one blank.

Voltage Divider

Vout = Vin × R2 / (R1 + R2)

RC Time Constant

τ = R × C. A capacitor charges to about 63% of the supply in one τ, and is nearly full after 5τ.

Power Dissipation

P = V × I, and also P = I² × R. Check your resistor's power rating against this.
